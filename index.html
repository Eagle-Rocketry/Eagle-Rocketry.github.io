EAGLE ROCKETRY

Students building flight hardware.

Cal State LA's Eagle Rocketry organization brings together students across engineering disciplines to design, manufacture, test, and fly aerospace systems. The university describes the organization as an SEDS chapter and lists rocket development among its student competition projects.
