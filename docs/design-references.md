# Website design references

This document preserves the visual direction selected for the Eagle Rocketry website. The links are listed in the original preference order, with the strongest reference first.

## Reference websites

1. [Flight 12](https://flight12.vercel.app/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
2. [United States Space Force](https://www.spaceforce.com/)
3. [Orbitline — Heron Ascent Story](https://animatedwebs.com/demos/orbitline-heron-ascent-story/index.html)
4. [Portal Space Systems](https://www.portalsystems.space/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

These websites are references for overall experience and art direction. Their code, layouts, text, images, and proprietary assets should not be copied.

## Approved design direction

- Create an immersive aerospace experience rather than a generic student-club template.
- Use a dark, technical visual system with restrained Cal State LA gold accents.
- Keep the opening readiness counter and rocket-launch reveal.
- Make launch smoke originate from the engine and spread from the launch point.
- Use scroll-linked movement, depth, transitions, and interactive diagrams to keep visitors engaged.
- Prefer real Eagle Rocketry diagrams, photographs, video, and team-owned CAD over fictional or AI-generated artwork.
- Keep the homepage cinematic and concise; place detailed engineering material on dedicated project pages.
- Preserve clear mobile navigation, reduced-motion support, readable text, and a visible way to skip animation.

## Current implementation

- The homepage introduces the program, systems, history, and engineering resources.
- The Liquid Systems page presents feed architecture and pressure-control logic.
- The Solid Rocket and TVC page presents onboard systems and two-servo motor control.
- Technical diagrams provide zoom, pan, and reset controls.
- The Spring 2026 bill of materials remains on SharePoint.
- The unfinished liquid control-panel ladder logic is labeled as work in progress.
- BPS.Space is credited through its official product page; copied commercial downloads are not promoted by the public site.

## Next visual milestone

The next major upgrade is an authentic interactive 3D vehicle or gimbal experience. It should begin only after the team supplies an approved, team-owned CAD assembly or web-ready model. Useful source formats include STEP, SolidWorks assemblies, Fusion 360 exports, and GLB/GLTF.

The team should also collect approved hardware photographs, launch photographs, test footage, project names, and dates. These assets will improve the website more than additional placeholder graphics.
